# Acadia National Park — Seasonal Closures and Access Notice, 2026

**Document No.:** ACAD-CL-2026-01
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 (last revised June 1, 2026)
**Issuing Office:** Office of the Superintendent, Acadia National Park
**Status:** Living document; revised as conditions change.

## 1. Purpose

This notice consolidates road, trail, and area closures and timed-access requirements in effect within Acadia National Park for 2026, issued under 36 CFR 1.5. Camping and fire restrictions appear in ACAD-CG-2025-02; carriage road and trail use rules in ACAD-CR-2025-05.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Vehicle reservation"** means a timed entry reservation purchased on Recreation.gov for the Cadillac Summit Road.
- **"Winter closure"** means the annual December 1 through April 14 closure of specified park roads to motor vehicles.
- **"Nesting closure"** means a wildlife closure under Section 4 protecting cliff-nesting raptors.

## 3. Roads and Vehicle Reservations

3.1 **Cadillac Summit Road:** vehicle reservations are required for all private vehicles from May 20 through October 22, 2026, during all hours the road is open. Thirty percent of reservations are released 90 days ahead; seventy percent at 10:00 a.m. Eastern Time two days ahead. Walking and bicycling up the Summit Road require no reservation. The Summit Road is closed to all vehicles December 1 through April 14 and during posted high-wind and ice events in the shoulder seasons.

3.2 **Park Loop Road:** closed to motor vehicles December 1, 2025 through April 14, 2026, except the Ocean Drive segment (Sand Beach entrance to Otter Cliff Road) and the Jordan Pond access road, which remain open year-round, conditions permitting. Full loop reopened April 15, 2026.

3.3 **Schoodic Loop Road:** open year-round, weather permitting; one-way traffic pattern in effect at all times from Frazer Point.

3.4 **Seasonal gate schedule:** unpaved fire roads (Hio Road, Marshall Brook, Long Pond fire roads) are gated December 1 through spring grading, typically mid-May.

## 4. Nesting Closures (Recurring)

4.1 Precipice Trail, Jordan Cliffs Trail, Valley Cove Trail, and the east face of Champlain Mountain: closed effective March 15, 2026, for peregrine falcon nesting. The Precipice and Champlain east face closures were lifted August 5 in 2025; 2026 lifting will be announced by press release and posted at trailheads when chicks fledge.

4.2 Petit Manan and outer island seabird colonies (Schoodic district waters): landing prohibited April 1 through August 31 annually.

4.3 Sand Beach nesting area (piping plover, when present): symbolic fencing may close portions of the upper beach April through August; obey posted boundaries.

## 5. Trail Closures — Temporary

5.1 Witch Hole Pond carriage road segment (posts 2–4): closed weekdays June 8 through July 3, 2026, for culvert replacement; open weekends.

5.2 Ocean Path (Thunder Hole to Otter Point): single-lane pedestrian pattern in effect through June 2026 during rehabilitation of storm-damaged sections from the January 2024 coastal storms; expect flagger control on weekdays.

5.3 Bass Harbor Head Light Station grounds: open 9:00 a.m. to sunset; the parking area closes when full, typically by 4:00 p.m. in summer for sunset viewing. No roadside parking on Lighthouse Road.

## 6. Mud Season

6.1 Carriage roads were closed to bicycles and equestrians March 12 through April 24, 2026, for spring thaw, under the authority described in ACAD-CR-2025-05, Section 6. All segments reopened April 25, 2026.

## 7. Water and Island Access

7.1 Echo Lake Beach and Sand Beach lifeguard season: June 20 through Labor Day 2026. Swimming outside lifeguard hours is at the visitor's own risk; water temperatures rarely exceed 60 °F at Sand Beach.

7.2 Duck Harbor (Isle au Haut) mailboat service: May 15 through October 12, 2026. Day-use visitor numbers to the Isle au Haut district are capped under the Compendium; boat capacity effectively enforces the cap.

7.3 Lake Wood and Half Moon Pond road access: closed to vehicles in 2026 due to parking area rehabilitation; walk-in access remains open.

## 8. Winter Operations Summary

8.1 December 1, 2026 forward: Park Loop Road (except Ocean Drive segment), Cadillac Summit Road, and fire roads close to vehicles; Blackwoods Campground converts to walk-in winter permit camping per ACAD-CG-2025-02, Section 7; the Hulls Cove Visitor Center closes for the season November 1, with winter information available at Park Headquarters on Eagle Lake Road.
