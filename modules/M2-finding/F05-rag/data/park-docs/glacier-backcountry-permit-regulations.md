# Glacier National Park — Backcountry Permit Regulations, 2026 Season

**Document No.:** GLAC-PERM-2026-01
**Effective Date:** January 15, 2026
**Issuing Office:** Backcountry Office, Glacier National Park
**Supersedes:** GLAC-PERM-2025-01

## 1. General Requirement

1.1 A backcountry permit is required for all overnight stays outside designated frontcountry campgrounds, year-round. This includes all designated backcountry campgrounds, the Sperry and Granite Park chalet-area campsites, undesignated-camping zones, and winter snow camping.

1.2 The permit must be carried by the trip leader for the duration of the trip and produced upon request of any ranger. Camping conduct under a permit is governed by GLAC-BC-2025-04, *Backcountry Camping Guide*.

1.3 A permit authorizes only the itinerary printed on it: named campsites, specific dates, party size, and entry trailhead. Deviation from the itinerary, except in an emergency, voids the permit.

## 2. Advance Reservations — 2026 Lottery

2.1 Advance reservations for the 2026 season (trips beginning June 15 through September 30, 2026) are allocated by lottery. The lottery application period runs March 1 through March 15, 2026, via Recreation.gov. Applications received outside this window are not considered.

2.2 Lottery results are issued by March 31, 2026. Successful applicants receive a 72-hour window to confirm an itinerary and pay the reservation fee ($40 per trip, non-refundable) plus the per-person nightly fee ($7 per person per night).

2.3 Each applicant may submit one application listing up to four alternative itineraries in order of preference. Duplicate applications by the same person or on behalf of the same party are disqualified.

2.4 Approximately 70 percent of each campground's capacity is released through the lottery; the remainder is held for walk-up issuance under Section 3.

2.5 Unclaimed lottery inventory is released to general on-line sale on April 15, 2026.

## 3. Walk-Up Permits

3.1 Walk-up permits are issued in person no earlier than 24 hours before the trip start date, at the Apgar Backcountry Permit Center, the St. Mary Visitor Center, the Many Glacier Ranger Station, or the Two Medicine Ranger Station, during posted hours.

3.2 Walk-up issuance is first-come, first-served. During July and August, visitors should expect lines before opening at the Apgar center and should prepare several alternative itineraries.

3.3 Walk-up permits carry the same fees as reserved permits, excluding the $40 reservation fee.

3.4 A walk-up permit may cover a trip of up to six nights. Extensions are granted only in person and only where capacity exists.

## 4. Party Size

4.1 The maximum party size on any backcountry permit is eight (8) persons. Parties of nine or more must split into independent groups holding separate permits, camping at different campgrounds on each night of the trip, and may not travel or cook as a single unit.

4.2 Certain campgrounds designated "small-party" on the backcountry map accept a maximum party size of four. The itinerary-building tools on Recreation.gov enforce these limits automatically; walk-up applicants should verify limits with the issuing ranger.

4.3 Each designated campground contains a limited number of tent sites; a party of eight may be required to occupy two adjacent sites at the discretion of the issuing office.

## 5. Itinerary Constraints

5.1 Maximum stay is three consecutive nights at any one backcountry campground, and 14 total backcountry nights per trip.

5.2 The first night's campsite must be reachable from the entry trailhead within the stated day's travel; the Backcountry Office may refuse itineraries it judges infeasible, including itineraries transiting trails closed under GLAC-CL-2026-01, *Seasonal Closures and Trail Status*. As of the June 2026 revision of that document, itineraries relying on the Avalanche Lake Trail segment cannot be issued.

5.3 Continental Divide Trail and Pacific Northwest Trail through-hikers may obtain a specialized through-hiker permit under a separate quota; see the Park website.

## 6. Permit Conditions

6.1 All permit holders must view the backcountry orientation (in person or via the online video, completed within seven days before the trip) covering food storage, fire rules, and wildlife requirements, including the advisories in GLAC-WL-2026-02, *Bear Safety Advisory*.

6.2 Permits may be cancelled or itineraries modified by the Park at any time in response to fire, flood, wildlife activity, or other hazard. Where the Park cancels a night, the per-person nightly fee for that night is refunded; the reservation fee is not.

6.3 No-shows forfeit the entire itinerary two hours after the permit center closes on the trip start date.

## 7. Winter Permits

7.1 For trips between December 1 and April 30, permits are issued free of charge, in person only, at Park Headquarters in West Glacier, up to seven days in advance. Winter campers must demonstrate avalanche awareness for itineraries crossing posted avalanche terrain.

## 8. Enforcement and Appeals

8.1 Camping without a valid permit, exceeding party size, or deviating from a permitted itinerary is punishable under 36 CFR 1.6 and 2.10.

8.2 Denials of walk-up itineraries on feasibility grounds under Section 5.2 may be appealed to the Backcountry Office supervisor on duty; the supervisor's decision is final.

## 9. Contact

Backcountry Office: (406) 888-7857. Lottery questions: Recreation.gov help line. Status of individual campgrounds is published in GLAC-CL-2026-01 and at permit centers.
