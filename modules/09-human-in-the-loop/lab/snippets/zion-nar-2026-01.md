Excerpt from ZION-NAR-2026-01, Zion National Park, Narrows Trip Planning and Permit Guide (effective February 1, 2026):

Section 2 (Definitions): "Bottom-up day use" means travel upstream from the Temple of Sinawava, no farther than Big Spring, returning the same day.

Section 3.1: Bottom-up day use to Big Spring requires no permit. Travel upstream of Big Spring without a top-down permit is prohibited.

Section 3.2: All top-down through-hikes require a wilderness permit, whether completed in one day or with an overnight at a Narrows campsite.

Section 6.2: All Narrows travel, including bottom-up day use, is closed automatically upon issuance of a flash flood warning by the National Weather Service for the North Fork drainage.
