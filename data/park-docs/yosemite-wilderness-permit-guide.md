# Yosemite National Park — Wilderness Permit and Trip Planning Guide

**Document No.:** YOSE-WILD-2026-01
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026
**Issuing Office:** Wilderness Division, Yosemite National Park
**Supersedes:** YOSE-WILD-2025-01

## 1. General Requirement

1.1 A wilderness permit is required year-round for all overnight stays in the Yosemite Wilderness. Day hiking does not require a permit, with the exception of the Half Dome cables, which require a Half Dome permit for day use.

1.2 Permits are issued per trailhead and entry date under a quota system. The permit must be carried by the trip leader and produced on request.

1.3 Fire use on wilderness trips is governed exclusively by YOSE-FIRE-2025-02, *Campfire and Stove Regulations*; food storage requirements appear in Section 5 of this guide.

## 2. Reservations

2.1 Sixty percent of each trailhead quota is reservable by weekly lottery on Recreation.gov, opening 24 weeks (168 days) before the entry date and running for one week. Remaining reservable inventory after each lottery is available first-come on-line until seven days before entry.

2.2 The reservation fee is $10 per confirmation plus $5 per person. Fees are non-refundable.

2.3 Forty percent of each quota is held for in-person issuance beginning at 11:00 a.m. the day before entry at the wilderness centers in Yosemite Valley, Tuolumne Meadows, Wawona, Big Oak Flat, and Hetch Hetchy.

2.4 Half Dome ascent by wilderness permit holders requires the Half Dome rider requested with the reservation ($10 per person if awarded).

## 3. Itinerary Rules

3.1 The permit fixes the entry trailhead and date and the first night's camping zone. Subsequent nights are flexible within the Wilderness, subject to the closures in YOSE-CL-2026-01, *Seasonal Closures and Access Notice*.

3.2 The first night must be spent at least four trail miles from any trailhead, and outside the no-camping zones posted for the Little Yosemite Valley corridor except within the designated Little Yosemite Valley campground.

3.3 Camping is prohibited within 100 feet of any water body or trail, above the posted treeline camping limits, and within one mile of any road or developed area.

3.4 Maximum group size is 15 persons on trail, and 8 persons for any party traveling off-trail or camping in a cross-country zone.

3.5 Maximum stay is 14 consecutive nights in the Wilderness, and no more than 2 consecutive nights in the Little Yosemite Valley campground.

## 4. Trailhead Access and Parking

4.1 Overnight parking is authorized only in signed backpacker lots at each trailhead; the permit number must be displayed on the dashboard.

4.2 Permit holders may spend the night before and the night after their trip in the backpacker campgrounds (Valley, Tuolumne Meadows, Wawona, Hetch Hetchy) for the posted nightly fee, one night on each end, without a separate campground reservation.

4.3 Holders entering via Tuolumne Meadows should note that Tioga Road typically opens between late May and early July; permits for entries before the road opening are issued conditionally.

## 5. Food Storage

5.1 An allowed bear-resistant food container is required for all overnight stays in the Yosemite Wilderness, parkwide, year-round. Counterbalance hanging does not satisfy this requirement anywhere in the Park.

5.2 All food, trash, and scented items must be stored in the container whenever they are more than an arm's reach away, including while sleeping and during side trips.

5.3 Containers must be from the Park's allowed list as of the trip date; rental containers are available at wilderness centers for a nominal fee with deposit.

5.4 In the Little Yosemite Valley and Merced Lake corridors, NPS food lockers, where present, may be used in lieu of containers while camped at those sites.

## 6. Sanitation

6.1 Human waste must be buried six inches deep at least 100 feet from water, camps, and trails. In the Mt. Whitney-style restricted zones posted at Little Yosemite Valley, toilets must be used where provided.

6.2 All toilet paper and hygiene products must be packed out. Burning toilet paper is prohibited (see YOSE-FIRE-2025-02, Section 7).

6.3 Washing with soap, including biodegradable soap, directly in any water body is prohibited.

## 7. Special Areas

7.1 Half Dome cables: ascent beyond the base of the subdome without a Half Dome permit is prohibited when the cables are up (typically the Friday before Memorial Day through the second Monday of October).

7.2 Hetch Hetchy: entry hours are limited to the posted gate schedule; overnight parties must exit itineraries accordingly. Swimming in Hetch Hetchy Reservoir is prohibited.

7.3 Sequoia groves (Mariposa, Merced, Tuolumne): overnight camping is prohibited within all grove boundaries.

## 8. Winter Use

8.1 From approximately November through May, quota requirements are relaxed and permits are self-issued at trailhead kiosks or obtained at open wilderness centers. Ski and snowshoe parties bound for the Ostrander Ski Hut reserve through the Yosemite Conservancy under a separate system.

8.2 Winter parties must still comply with container requirements under Section 5 and with YOSE-FIRE-2025-02.

## 9. Enforcement

Camping without a permit, exceeding group size, or violating food-storage requirements is punishable under 36 CFR 1.6, 2.10, and 2.13. Wilderness rangers patrol year-round.

## 10. Contact

Wilderness information: (209) 372-0740. Same-day permit availability is posted each morning at wilderness centers and on the Park website. See YOSE-FAQ-2026-01 for plain-language answers to common questions.
