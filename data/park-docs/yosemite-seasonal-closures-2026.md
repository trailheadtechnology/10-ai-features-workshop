# Yosemite National Park — Seasonal Closures and Access Notice, 2026

**Document No.:** YOSE-CL-2026-01
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026 (last revised June 15, 2026)
**Issuing Office:** Office of the Superintendent, Yosemite National Park
**Status:** Living document, revised as conditions change.

## 1. Purpose

This notice consolidates closures and access restrictions in effect within Yosemite National Park for calendar year 2026, issued under 36 CFR 1.5. Entry into a closed area is prohibited. Fire-related restrictions are issued separately under YOSE-FIRE-2025-02, Section 6, and are not repeated here except where they close an area entirely.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Seasonal closure"** means a closure recurring annually on approximately fixed dates.
- **"Conditional opening"** means an opening date dependent on snowpack, plowing progress, or hazard assessment.
- **"Restoration closure"** means a closure protecting revegetation or cultural resources, of indefinite duration.

## 3. Roads

3.1 Tioga Road: closed for winter as of November 24, 2025; plowing began April 13, 2026; opened to through traffic June 1, 2026.

3.2 Glacier Point Road: opened May 16, 2026. Nighttime closures (10:00 p.m. to 6:00 a.m.) apply Tuesday and Wednesday nights through July for culvert work between Badger Pass and Bridalveil Creek.

3.3 Mariposa Grove Road: closed to private vehicles year-round; access by shuttle from the Welcome Plaza (March 28 through November 30, 2026) or on foot.

3.4 Hetch Hetchy Road: open daily 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. through Labor Day, shorter hours thereafter. The gate schedule constrains wilderness itineraries; see YOSE-WILD-2026-01, Section 7.2.

## 4. Trails — Temporary Closures

4.1 Mist Trail (Vernal Fall corridor): the winter route closure was lifted April 10, 2026. The trail closes each winter when ice accumulates on the steps; the John Muir Trail serves as the winter route.

4.2 Four Mile Trail: lower section closed for rockfall mitigation May 4 through June 12, 2026; fully open as of June 13, 2026.

4.3 Snow Creek Trail: switchback section under restoration closure following the 2025 debris flow; closed until further notice. Through-travel between Mirror Lake and the North Rim is not possible on this route; use the Yosemite Falls or Porcupine Creek approaches.

4.4 Wapama Falls footbridges (Hetch Hetchy): closed intermittently during high flow when water overtops the bridges, typically May and June. Closure status is posted at the O'Shaughnessy Dam kiosk each morning.

## 5. Trails and Areas — Seasonal (Recurring)

5.1 Half Dome cables: raised for the season May 22, 2026; scheduled removal October 12, 2026. Ascent above the subdome without a permit is prohibited while cables are up; ascent when cables are down is strongly discouraged and requires technical equipment.

5.2 El Capitan: portions of the southeast face are closed March 1 through July 15 annually for peregrine falcon nesting. Climbing route closures are posted at the El Capitan bridge kiosk.

5.3 Badger Pass Ski Area: winter operations December through March, conditions permitting; the area is closed to snowmobiles at all times.

5.4 Bridalveil Fall plunge pool: talus scrambling beyond the viewing platform is prohibited year-round.

## 6. Camping-Related Closures

6.1 Little Yosemite Valley campground: open May 15 through October 15, 2026. Camping in the corridor outside the designated campground is prohibited (see YOSE-WILD-2026-01, Section 3.2).

6.2 Merced Lake shoreline zone: camping and fires prohibited within the posted shoreline restoration boundary; the established High Sierra Camp area sites remain available.

6.3 Tuolumne Meadows Campground: reopened for the 2026 season on June 19, 2026, following the multi-year rehabilitation project, at reduced capacity (approximately 60 percent of former sites).

## 7. Wildlife Closures

7.1 Ahwahnee Meadow and Cook's Meadow: off-boardwalk entry prohibited year-round (restoration).

7.2 Bridalveil Creek riparian corridor above the fall: closed April 1 through August 15, 2026, for willow flycatcher habitat.

7.3 Isolated bear-activity closures of individual campsites or food-locker areas may be posted on site with immediate effect and are not individually listed in this document.

## 8. Winter Operations Summary

8.1 From approximately November through March: Tioga and Glacier Point roads closed (Glacier Point Road plowed to Badger Pass); Mist Trail winter route in effect; wilderness permits self-issued (YOSE-WILD-2026-01, Section 8); tire chains may be required on any park road at any time, including for four-wheel-drive vehicles.
