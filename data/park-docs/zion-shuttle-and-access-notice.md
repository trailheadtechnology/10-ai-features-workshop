# Zion National Park — Shuttle Operations and Canyon Access Notice, 2026

**Document No.:** ZION-SH-2026-01
**Effective Date:** February 14, 2026
**Issuing Office:** Division of Visitor and Resource Protection, Zion National Park

## 1. Purpose

This notice establishes the 2026 operating framework for the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive shuttle system and the associated private-vehicle restriction, issued under 36 CFR 1.5 and 4.30. It also summarizes access rules for the east side of the Park and the Kolob Canyons district.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Shuttle season"** means February 14 through November 29, 2026, inclusive.
- **"Scenic Drive"** means the Zion Canyon Scenic Drive from Canyon Junction to the Temple of Sinawava.
- **"Springdale Line"** means the town shuttle operating between Springdale and the Zion Canyon Visitor Center pedestrian entrance.
- **"Canyon Line"** means the park shuttle operating along the Scenic Drive with nine stops.

## 3. Private Vehicle Restriction

3.1 During shuttle season, the Scenic Drive is closed to private vehicles. Access to all Scenic Drive stops, including the Grotto (Angels Landing), Weeping Rock, and the Temple of Sinawava (Riverside Walk and Narrows), is by Canyon Line shuttle, bicycle, or foot.

3.2 The following are exempt from Section 3.1: registered guests of Zion Lodge, to the Lodge only; holders of accessibility placards issued at the visitor center, subject to daily availability; and administrative and emergency vehicles.

3.3 Outside shuttle season (November 30, 2026 through mid-February 2027), private vehicles may use the Scenic Drive; parking at trailhead lots is first-come and fills by mid-morning on holiday weekends.

## 4. Shuttle Operations

4.1 Canyon Line service operates daily during shuttle season, beginning at 6:00 a.m. from the visitor center, with last departure from the Temple of Sinawava at 8:15 p.m. (7:15 p.m. before March 14 and after October 25). Frequency is 5 to 10 minutes midday.

4.2 No tickets or reservations are required for either line. Boarding is first-come at each stop.

4.3 Oversize items are limited to one item per passenger not exceeding 48 inches; canyoneering packs and Narrows staffs are permitted. Bear spray, firearms carried per applicable law, and fuel canisters must be secured in packs.

4.4 Pets are not permitted on shuttles, with the exception of service animals. This effectively limits visitors with pets to the Pa'rus Trail and areas outside the Scenic Drive during shuttle season.

4.5 Hikers exiting the Narrows after the final Canyon Line departure face a 8.5-mile walk to Canyon Junction; through-hike permit holders are advised to plan exits no later than two hours before final departure. See ZION-NAR-2026-01, Section 4.2.

## 5. Bicycles

5.1 Bicycles, including class 1 e-bikes, are permitted on the Scenic Drive and the Pa'rus Trail year-round. Class 2 and 3 e-bikes are restricted to the roadway.

5.2 Cyclists on the Scenic Drive must pull to the shoulder and place a foot on the ground when a shuttle approaches from behind; shuttles do not pass moving cyclists in the narrow segments.

5.3 Bicycles may not be ridden on any trail other than the Pa'rus, nor carried on any route requiring their portage over slickrock.

## 6. East Side and Kolob Canyons Access

6.1 The Zion–Mount Carmel Highway (State Route 9) remains open to private vehicles year-round. Vehicles exceeding tunnel size limits (7 feet 10 inches in width or 11 feet 4 inches in height, including mirrors and accessories) require a tunnel escort permit ($15, valid for two passages), with escorted passage available 8:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. daily during 2026.

6.2 The Canyon Overlook Trail parking area holds approximately 30 vehicles; roadside parking outside marked spaces on Route 9 is prohibited and enforced by citation and towing.

6.3 Kolob Canyons Road (accessed from Interstate 15, exit 40) is open year-round, weather permitting, with winter closures after snowfall until plowed. The Kolob district has no shuttle and no fuel, food, or lodging services.

6.4 Kolob Terrace Road, serving the Subway trailheads and Lava Point, is not plowed above the Park boundary in winter; Lava Point Campground typically operates May through September. Camping and fire rules for Lava Point appear in ZION-BC-2025-03, Section 4.3.

## 7. Parking and Congestion Management

7.1 The visitor center lot typically fills by 8:00 a.m. from March through October. Visitors arriving later should park in Springdale (municipal fee lots) and use the Springdale Line, entering the Park on foot at the pedestrian bridge.

7.2 Stopping, standing, or parking on the roadway shoulder of Route 9 within Park boundaries outside marked areas is prohibited at all times.

## 8. Relationship to Other Documents

Permit-related transportation constraints for the Narrows are addressed in ZION-NAR-2026-01. Backcountry conduct, including the parkwide backcountry fire prohibition, is governed by ZION-BC-2025-03. Plain-language visitor guidance appears in ZION-FAQ-2026-01.

## 9. Contact

Shuttle status and schedules: (435) 772-3256, or posted at the visitor center. During high-wind events, shuttle service may be suspended without notice; monitor posted advisories.
