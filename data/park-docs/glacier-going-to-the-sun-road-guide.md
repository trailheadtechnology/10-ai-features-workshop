# Glacier National Park — Going-to-the-Sun Road Corridor Guide

**Document No.:** GLAC-VS-2025-07
**Effective Date:** June 1, 2025 (revised May 15, 2026)
**Issuing Office:** Division of Visitor Services, Glacier National Park

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This guide describes access rules, vehicle limitations, shuttle operations, and visitor services along the Going-to-the-Sun Road (the "Road") between the West Glacier entrance and the St. Mary entrance. It supplements, and does not replace, the closure and status information published in GLAC-CL-2026-01, *Seasonal Closures and Trail Status*, and the entry-reservation requirements summarized in GLAC-FAQ-2026-01.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Alpine section"** means the segment of the Road between Avalanche Creek and Rising Sun, including Logan Pass.
- **"Corridor"** means the Road plus all pullouts, trailheads, and developed areas accessed directly from it.
- **"Oversize vehicle"** means any vehicle or vehicle combination exceeding 21 feet in length (including bumpers), 8 feet in width (including mirrors), or 10 feet in height.

## 3. Vehicle Restrictions

3.1 Oversize vehicles are prohibited on the alpine section at all times. The restriction is enforced at gates at Avalanche Creek (westbound limit) and Rising Sun (eastbound limit). Vehicles between 21 and 26 feet may travel the Road only below these points.

3.2 Bicycles are prohibited on the alpine section between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. from June 15 through Labor Day, in both directions, due to limited sight lines and shoulder width.

3.3 Overnight parking is prohibited at Logan Pass and at the Avalanche area lots except by holders of backcountry permits whose itineraries are annotated for overnight parking by the issuing office (see GLAC-PERM-2026-01, Section 6).

3.4 Parking outside marked spaces, including on vegetation or in traffic lanes, is prohibited and subject to towing at the owner's expense.

## 4. Seasonal Operation

4.1 The lower portions of the Road (West Glacier to Avalanche; St. Mary to Rising Sun or Jackson Glacier Overlook) are generally open year-round to the winter closure gates, conditions permitting.

4.2 Spring plowing of the alpine section typically begins in April; opening dates have historically ranged from early June to mid-July. For 2026, the alpine section opened June 27. Fall closure typically occurs on the third Monday of October or upon the first sustained snowfall, whichever is earlier.

4.3 During spring plowing, the Road above the vehicle closure is open to hikers and bicyclists on weekends as posted, at their own risk. Avalanche hazard in the Road corridor persists into June.

## 5. Shuttle System

5.1 A free shuttle operates along the Road from July 1 through Labor Day, 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., with express and local patterns from the Apgar Visitor Center (west) and the St. Mary Visitor Center (east), meeting at Logan Pass.

5.2 Shuttles are first-come, first-served; no reservations. Midday waits at Avalanche and Logan Pass commonly exceed 45 minutes in peak season.

5.3 Riding the shuttle does not exempt a private vehicle from corridor entry-reservation requirements when that vehicle enters the corridor; however, visitors parking outside the corridor (Apgar or St. Mary) and riding the shuttle do not need a vehicle reservation.

5.4 Note that while the Avalanche shuttle stop remains in service, the Avalanche Lake Trail beyond the Trail of the Cedars junction is closed until further notice per GLAC-CL-2026-01, Section 4.1. Shuttle riders should plan for Trail of the Cedars only at that stop.

5.5 Bear spray may be carried aboard shuttles only in a manufacturer's holster with the safety clip in place.

## 6. Trailheads Served

6.1 Principal corridor trailheads, west to east: Apgar lookout spur, Sperry Trailhead (at Lake McDonald Lodge, the recommended approach to the Sperry Chalet area; see GLAC-CL-2026-01, Section 6.1), Trail of the Cedars/Avalanche, The Loop (Granite Park), Logan Pass (Hidden Lake Overlook and Highline), Jackson Glacier Overlook (Gunsight Pass), St. Mary and Virginia Falls, and Rising Sun.

6.2 The Highline Trail from Logan Pass may be closed in early season for snow; status is posted at the Logan Pass Visitor Center daily.

6.3 Hikers beginning overnight trips from corridor trailheads must display their backcountry permit on the dashboard where overnight parking is authorized.

## 7. Services and Facilities

7.1 Potable water: Apgar, Lake McDonald Lodge, Avalanche (seasonal), Logan Pass (seasonal, limited), Rising Sun, St. Mary Visitor Center.

7.2 Fuel is not available anywhere within the Park. The nearest stations are in West Glacier and St. Mary.

7.3 Food service operates seasonally at Lake McDonald Lodge and Rising Sun. There is no food service at Logan Pass.

7.4 Restrooms at Logan Pass operate on a reduced-capacity basis before July 1 and after September 15.

## 8. Weather and Safety

8.1 Weather on the alpine section can change from summer to winter conditions within an hour in any month. Visitors should carry layered clothing on all Logan Pass area trails.

8.2 Mountain goats and bighorn sheep frequent the Logan Pass area. Feeding or approaching within 25 yards is prohibited under 36 CFR 2.2; see GLAC-WL-2026-02 for wildlife requirements generally.

8.3 Rockfall zones between The Loop and Logan Pass are signed. Stopping in signed rockfall zones is prohibited.
