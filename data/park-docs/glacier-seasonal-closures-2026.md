# Glacier National Park — Seasonal Closures and Trail Status, 2026 Season

**Document No.:** GLAC-CL-2026-01
**Effective Date:** May 1, 2026 (last revised June 24, 2026)
**Issuing Office:** Office of the Superintendent, Glacier National Park
**Status:** Living document; revised as conditions change. Verify current status at any visitor center before travel.

## 1. Purpose

This notice consolidates seasonal closures, temporary closures, and reduced-service designations in effect for the 2026 visitor season. Closures listed here are issued under the authority of 36 CFR 1.5 and the Superintendent's Compendium. Entry into a closed area is prohibited and punishable by fine.

Closures related to bear activity are additionally described in GLAC-WL-2026-02, *Bear Safety Advisory*. Backcountry campground status affecting permit itineraries is coordinated with the Backcountry Office under GLAC-PERM-2026-01.

## 2. Definitions

- **"Closed"** means all public entry is prohibited.
- **"Restricted"** means entry is permitted subject to posted conditions (e.g., groups of four or more, no off-trail travel).
- **"Until further notice"** means the closure has no scheduled end date and will be lifted by a subsequent revision of this document.

## 3. Road Status

3.1 Going-to-the-Sun Road: the alpine section between Avalanche Creek and Rising Sun opened to vehicles on June 27, 2026. Overnight parking at Logan Pass is prohibited. See GLAC-VS-2025-07, *Going-to-the-Sun Road Corridor Guide*, for vehicle size limits and shuttle information.

3.2 Many Glacier Road: open, with 30-minute delays possible at the construction zone near mile 3 through August 2026.

3.3 Inside North Fork Road: open to Polebridge entrance; high-clearance vehicles recommended north of Bowman Lake junction.

3.4 Two Medicine Road: open for the season as of May 23, 2026.

## 4. Trail Closures — Temporary

4.1 **Avalanche Lake Trail: CLOSED effective June 20, 2026, until further notice.** The footbridge over Avalanche Creek approximately 0.4 miles above the Trail of the Cedars junction washed out during high runoff in mid-June 2026. The trail is closed from the Trail of the Cedars junction to Avalanche Lake in both directions. The Trail of the Cedars loop itself remains open. Engineering assessment of the bridge abutments is underway; a replacement schedule has not been established. Visitors holding backcountry itineraries that transit this segment should contact the Backcountry Office for re-routing.

4.2 **Hidden Meadow Trail: RESTRICTED effective June 26, 2026.** Due to sustained grizzly bear activity in the Hidden Meadow area reported in late June 2026, travel is permitted only in groups of four or more, and off-trail travel within one mile of the meadow is prohibited. See GLAC-WL-2026-02, Section 5, for details and required precautions. This restriction may be upgraded to a full closure without advance notice.

4.3 Swiftcurrent Pass Trail (upper switchbacks): closed for tread reconstruction Mondays through Thursdays, July 6 to July 30, 2026. Open Fridays through Sundays.

4.4 Gunsight Pass Trail: snowfield crossing at the pass rated hazardous; ice axe advised through mid-July. Not closed, but the Sperry Chalet approach from the Jackson Glacier Overlook side is not recommended for parties without mountaineering experience until the snowfield melts out.

## 5. Trail and Area Closures — Seasonal (Recurring)

5.1 Logan Pass area meadows: off-trail travel prohibited year-round for vegetation protection.

5.2 Apikuni Falls cliff base: closed annually February 1 through July 31 for raptor nesting.

5.3 Upper McDonald Creek: closed to all entry November 15 through March 1 annually for bald eagle wintering habitat.

5.4 Trail of the Cedars boardwalk: closed to bicycles and stock year-round.

## 6. Backcountry Campground Status

6.1 Sperry Chalet area campsites (SPE): open for the 2026 season as of July 1. Note that the Gunsight Pass approach carries the hazard advisory in Section 4.4; the Sperry Trail from Lake McDonald Lodge is the recommended route. Fire and stove rules for these sites are set out in GLAC-BC-2025-04, Section 4, and are not affected by seasonal fire-danger changes.

6.2 Fifty Mountain (FIF): opening delayed to July 15, 2026, due to lingering snowpack.

6.3 Cracker Lake (CRA): open; food hanging system at site 2 damaged, storage lockers in service.

6.4 Campgrounds reachable only via the Avalanche Lake Trail segment closed under Section 4.1 are unaffected (no designated backcountry campgrounds lie beyond the washout), but day access to Avalanche Lake is not possible until the bridge is replaced.

## 7. Waters

7.1 All Park waters remain subject to the non-motorized watercraft inspection requirement. Lake McDonald and St. Mary Lake inspection stations operate 7:00 a.m. to 8:30 p.m. daily through September 20, 2026.

7.2 Avalanche Creek is closed to all watercraft and swimming between the gorge and the lake outlet, a pre-existing closure unrelated to the 2026 bridge washout.
